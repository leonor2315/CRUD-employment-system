TELENERGY Employee Work System - Client Guide

1) Daily Use

  • Open: http://localhost:5173
  • Log in with your assigned username/password (admin users can access Form Designer and full views).
  • Use Reload Data when you need the latest records.
  • Use Employee List View and Employee Detail View to review records.

2) Employee Profile Entry

  • Fill required sections (Personal Info, Work Info, Benefits, etc.).
  • Upload identity images (passport, Ghana Card front/back).
  • Upload documents for Apparisal, Performance Contract, Certificates, and CV.
  • Certificates supports multiple uploads.

3) Export to PDF

  • Open an employee in Employee Detail View.
  • Click Export to PDF.
  • In the print dialog, choose Save as PDF and save/share the file.

4) Export All Employees (CSV)

  • Open Employee List View.
  • Click Export All (CSV).
  • Share the downloaded CSV in Excel-compatible format.

5) Location Management

  • Open Form Designer.
  • Use Location Stations section to add/remove stations.

6) Dashboard Metric Cards

  • Total Employees, Engaged Employees, and On Payroll are counted from active employee records.
  • If numbers look old, click Reload Data, then Ctrl+F5 if needed.
